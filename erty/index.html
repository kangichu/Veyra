Erty is property and tenant management software for landlords, property managers and agencies. It covers setting up buildings, estates and units, letting them, billing rent and taking payments, repairs, agents and the team who does the work.

Chapter 01 / What Erty does

Everything a property
needs, day to day.

Erty keeps the records behind a property portfolio in one system: the units, the people living or working in them, the money they pay and the work done on the buildings. Each part is built around how property is actually run.

  1. 01

    Properties & units

    Apartment blocks, single houses, housing estates and commercial spaces, in any mix. Houses can be offered for rent, for sale or both. A guided setup review checks every unit before letting starts.

  2. 02

    Tenants & rent

    Onboard tenants, bill rent in advance and record payments, which are applied to the oldest bill first, with any extra held as credit. Deposits, statements and receipts are included, and payments keep their M-Pesa and bank references.

  3. 03

    Maintenance & expenses

    Track every repair from the moment it is reported to the day it is closed, with who is handling it and what it cost. Property expenses are recorded against the property they belong to.

  4. 04

    Agents & commission

    Leads and follow-up tasks, agents responsible for whole properties or single units, and a commission ledger. Commission is earned on money actually collected, with statements and payouts.

  5. 05

    Team & access

    Roles grouped by department, from administrators to caretakers. Each person sees only what their role allows, and only the properties assigned to them, with notifications for what concerns them.

  6. 06

    Property history

    A timeline of each property: ownership, title documents, renovations, utilities, incidents and past sales. Records are kept as written, so the history can be trusted.

Chapter 02 / Optional modules

Turn on what you use.
Leave out what you don't.

The core manages properties and tenants. Three modules add more, and each can be switched off for the whole installation or for a single business.

Listings

Advertise what is free.

One listing per unit type, with rent, deposit and photos, and a count of how many units are vacant right now. A public listings API lets a website or app show them.

Sales

Sell houses, not just let them.

Record a buyer, an agreed price and a payment plan with a deposit and instalments. Every payment gets a receipt, agents earn sales commission as the buyer pays, and the house is marked sold on completion.

Plans & subscriptions

Offer Erty as a service.

Plans, checkout and usage limits for running Erty as a hosted service for other businesses. When you run Erty as your own system, switch it off and the limits go with it.

A few practical questions

Clear answers.
Before the release.

Is Erty available now?

Erty is built, and we are preparing it for an open-source release. It is not yet publicly downloadable. Ask to be notified to hear when it is released, or get in touch to discuss using it sooner.

When will it be open source, and under what licence?

There is no release date yet. The licence, the repository and the documentation will all be published with the release.

Can we host it ourselves?

Yes. Erty is a web application designed to run as your own system. The optional modules (Listings, Sales, and Plans & subscriptions) can be switched off when you don't need them.

Which kinds of property does it handle?

Apartment blocks, single houses and housing estates, commercial spaces such as shops and offices, and mixed-use properties that combine them. Commercial spaces can be let in parts by floor area.

Does it fit how rent works in Kenya?

Erty is built in Nairobi. Amounts are in Kenyan shillings by default, payments keep their M-Pesa and bank references, and buyer records include an ID number and KRA PIN.
