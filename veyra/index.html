A few practical questions

Clear answers.
Before you commit.

Which Domains are being developed?

Veyra is in active development, with Real Estate as its first Domain. Additional Domains require development and validation. Capability scope, including Advanced Intelligence, depends on the enabled Domain, configuration and commercial agreement.

Where does our data live?

Customer data, local representations, indexes and intelligence workloads stay in the customer-controlled Veyra environment. Your source data remains authoritative. Access and external connections are reviewed for your deployment.

How is Veyra deployed? Is it air-gapped?

Veyra uses Docker in customer-controlled infrastructure. V1 assumes one active engine-platform replica per installation. Your team manages TLS, hosting, networking, storage and local logs. Periodic Tandish communication validates licensing and rights; an offline grace mechanism exists, but permanent disconnection is not supported. Supported local models run locally after acquisition.

How does our application integrate?

Your backend connects to the local /v1 API using project API keys. Your team maps and imports data and presents results in your existing interface. Integration uses REST and JSON/CSV data flows; a generic database connector is not implied.

How are scope and responsibilities agreed?

Deployment and capability scope are tailored to the enabled Domain and commercial agreement. Your team controls infrastructure operations; Tandish retains licensing, entitlements, signed releases and update authority. Support arrangements are agreed with Tandish; no public SLA or fixed deployment timeline is defined.
