# Tandish homepage product gallery — 11 October 2026

1. **Old product structure:** hero → two product preview windows → full Veyra chapter → full Saelis chapter → approach → contact. Product details dominated the homepage and would add vertical length for every future product.
2. **New structure:** preserved Tandish hero → Our Products editorial gallery → Our Approach → contact → footer. The detail panel opens only when selected. Approximately 1.9 cards are visible on wide desktop, 1.65 on smaller desktop/tablet, and one substantial card plus the next edge on mobile.
3. **Old chapters removed:** both permanently expanded product chapters and the duplicate hero previews are deleted from the homepage. Their important content is condensed into the product detail interaction, with optional native disclosures as the fallback.
4. **Scalability:** semantic article attributes configure product identity, tone, link and detail content. JavaScript derives the product array and counts from those articles; adding another entry does not require another homepage chapter or changing a hard-coded product count. Native overflow with proximity snapping supports horizontal gestures. Arrow buttons and Arrow Left/Right, Home and End provide alternatives. No autoplay or wheel interception.
5. **Veyra panel:** platform → capabilities → Domains → current production Real Estate. Search, Recommendations, Price Intelligence and enabled Advanced Intelligence are explicitly shared capabilities. Concise illustrative Real Estate workflow; commercial/configuration qualification; no fake industry availability. Persistent CTA opens `/veyra/`.
6. **Saelis panel:** social discovery, coming-soon status, shared interests, real experiences and reasons to meet. Existing connection illustration and green product personality retained. Persistent CTA emails Tandish about Saelis.
7. **Previous/next:** gallery navigation is bounded, disabling unavailable directions. Drawer navigation wraps directly between configured products and updates its count and meaningful accessible labels. Last → Next returns to first; first → Previous returns to last. No close/reopen required.
8. **Transition:** desktop modal slides from the left in 380ms, occupying 66% of the viewport (70% at smaller desktop widths, maximum 980px). Subtle backdrop de-emphasizes the portfolio. Product switching uses an 18px / 260ms transition. Opening a panel does not modify the URL or add history entries; normal browser history remains authoritative. Veyra’s detailed canonical destination remains unchanged.
9. **Accessibility:** native modal dialog semantics and inert background; valid labelled heading; visible Close; Escape and outside-backdrop click close; explicit Tab cycling supplements native containment; focus returns to the originating card. Product switching focuses the new heading. Cards remain links usable with Enter, and modified clicks preserve their underlying destinations. Gallery controls have descriptive labels and a live navigation announcement.
10. **Mobile:** full-width sheet with one scrolling content area and locked background. Close and previous/next controls remain visible. Product CTA stays in the persistent footer, so visitors can reach it without scrolling to the bottom. Reopened panels start at their heading.
11. **Reduced motion:** carousel navigation becomes immediate; drawer and content-switch animations are disabled. The same content, controls and document order remain available.
12. **No-JS/failure:** cards are semantic links containing names, positioning, status and product context. Veyra links directly to `/veyra/`; Saelis retains its mailto. Native collapsed “More about” disclosures provide the concise additional material. Only after successful enhancement are those disclosures replaced by the dialog interaction. If native dialog is unavailable, this same fallback remains. No product information is solely generated by JavaScript.
13. **SEO:** Tandish remains the homepage subject and title. Product names and descriptions are visible in source. Veyra’s platform, capabilities, Domain model and first production Real Estate status appear directly in its card, ahead of any illustrative property context. The enhanced dialog supplements those facts.
14. **Structured data:** Organization, WebSite, homepage WebPage, the defined Veyra entity and Saelis entity retained. Veyra’s description replaces outdated plan-dependent language with enabled Domain/commercial agreement/configuration. No pricing/package metadata introduced. `/veyra` metadata and structured data unchanged.
15. **Length:** default homepage is about 37% shorter at 1440px and 47% shorter at 390/375px (table below). Length no longer grows by a full section for each product. A visitor can browse deeper details on demand.
