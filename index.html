Veyra

Self-hosted property search and recommendations for real-estate companies, agencies and developers.

Help customers and teams find relevant listings through your existing website, portal or internal tools, using data you already manage.

See how Veyra fits ↗

  • For property businesses.

    Give customers and property teams a way to describe what they need, instead of relying only on filters and repeated searches.

  • Fits your existing systems.

    Your developers connect your listing data and application backend to Veyra. Your team keeps control of the website, source data and hosting environment.

  • More ways to find a match.

    Add natural-language search, relevant results and property recommendations to help people explore and shortlist listings.
